YancoTV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

YancoTV is a media player. It plays content from playlists you configure.
We run no accounts, no analytics, and no servers of our own.

What the app stores

Everything the app knows lives on your device: your playlist sources,
channel lists and programme guide, favourites, watch history, resume points,
recordings and settings. Provider usernames and passwords are stored in your
device's hardware keystore, not in plain text. Nothing is uploaded to us —
we have nowhere to upload it to.

What the app sends, and to whom

- Your IPTV provider(s). The app contacts only the provider URLs you add,
  to fetch playlists, guide data and streams. Your credentials go to your
  provider — that is how IPTV login works — and to no one else.
- Crash reports (optional). If "Crash reports" is enabled in
  Settings → About, crash data (stack trace, device model, OS and app
  version) is sent to Sentry so bugs can be fixed. No playlists, no
  credentials, no viewing history. Provider URLs are scrubbed of credentials
  before any report leaves the device. Turn the toggle off and nothing is
  sent.
- Update checks. The app periodically fetches a small version file and,
  when you accept an update, the new APK from GitHub. This tells GitHub your
  IP address, as any download does.
- Subtitles (when you search). Subtitle searches go to OpenSubtitles with
  the title being searched.

What we never do

No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of data, no
reading of anything beyond what the features above need.

Your data, your device

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Backups you export are
encrypted with a password you choose and stay wherever you put them.
